## Exported hooks need unwrapped identity during teardown (2026-11-20 17:45, issue #261)

Disabling 1.17.2-beta closed the Player and retained the Position in
Zotero 10.0.9. The reader still had its own `loadVoices` method after
disable. Reopening the native popup left the Voice catalog empty.
Calling the inherited loader directly in the same fixture loaded the
two controlled native voices and resolved the remembered second one.

A separate bridge probe installed an exported function on an object in
the reader realm from a Zotero-style sandbox. Comparing the property
with the exported function by raw identity returned false. Waiving both
wrappers returned true. The live Voice catalog now makes ownership
checks this way before deleting `loadVoices` and `deactivate`. An
unrelated replacement still belongs to whoever installed it.
