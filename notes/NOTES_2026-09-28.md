## A skip called a pending voice switch off twice, and the Handoff never prepares behind the reading (2026-09-28 12:20, issue #154)

The owner reported that after a voice change whose new voice had no audio
yet, Left or Shift+Left kept the original voice. A unit loop at the
voice-pick seam (the manager shadow, `EngineSession` and `Handoff` on the
virtual clock, voice C's fetch held) reproduced it for both granularities:
notices `preparing:C, cancelled:C`, then `a:One two three.@0` — the old
voice replayed the target from its own cache, which a skip back almost
always hits. A plain skip with no switch pending stopped the old clip at
once (the control stayed green), so the symptom exists only while a switch
is pending, which an uncached new voice makes long enough to notice.

The switch was called off in two places: voice-pick's `CANCELLING` shadow
on `skipBack` / `skipAhead`, and `skipTo`'s own `handoff?.cancel()`.
Removing both (probed, then reverted) kept the switch alive but still read
the target in the old voice: the Handoff hands over only at a word of the
sentence playing or at the start of a later sentence it holds
(`sentenceStart` needs `ready.has(index)`), and its poll only prepares at or
ahead of the position. A sentence behind the reading is never prepared.

The skip now commits the switch first (`Handoff.commitNow`): the session
adopts the new voice and its clip store — prepared clips and requests in
flight included — without playing (`adoptVoice`), then the manager's own
`selectVoice` rebuilds a controller that carries on, and only then does the
manager's skip run, on that controller. The commit sits in voice-pick's
manager shadow, not in `skipTo`: from there the manager's `_createController`
(reader.js 82653) would run inside its own controller's `skipBack`
(82583-82588). `adoptVoice` drops the paused resume point: its offset is a
place in the old voice's clip, and a paused skip back from the first
sentence lands on that same sentence. `watchStart` now says `selected` at
once while paused; before, a commit that found the session paused polled
until the 120 s deadline and reported `failed`.

## Verified live: every skip takes the pending switch, and a word-timed pair never prepares ahead while playing (2026-09-28 16:02, issue #154)

`zotero-tester` verified 1.16.2-beta (xpi `c8a1c103…`, commit `fcfa451`)
on macOS with the Kokoro pair `af_bella` / `af_alloy`; the pending window
was a cold request to the configured Kokoro server. For trusted `←`,
`Shift+←`, `→`, `Shift+→` and the player's Skip to Previous Sentence button,
the diagnostics showed the commit before any new audio: session voice the
target, `handoff: null`, `skipPending: true`, `carriedOn` up one,
`started` flat, `last.kind: "skip"`; the new voice then read the target
from its start and the notice turned `selected`. Paused, the notice said
`selected` at once, nothing was requested before Play, and a `←` landing
on the sentence paused in (at 1.02 s) restarted it in the new voice. A
speed change still cancelled, and a switch without a skip still handed
over at a shared word.

Two things the run met. Two word-timed voices arm a word cut on the
sentence playing and never fill `handoff.prepared` ahead of it, so the
reuse of prepared audio could be observed only paused, where `armWord`
stands down; there the skip to the prepared sentence fetched nothing. And
a cancel leaves `voiceSwitch()`'s `last` at the previous switch's
boundary: only `pending`, `stage` and the notice change.
