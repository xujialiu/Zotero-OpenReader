## A provider that cannot list reached the Player as a voice that is gone (2026-10-14 11:45, issue #179)

When a document voice's provider could not list its voices at a Player
open, the Player said the saved voice was unavailable and to choose
another — advice that overwrites the document voice (ADR 0009) for a
failure that passes. The reason was lost at three points, each measured
on a composition of the real Fish Audio provider, `collectCatalog`, the
remote interface and memory-sync, with `fetch` rejecting as Gecko's does
offline (`TypeError: NetworkError when attempting to fetch resource.`):

- `collectCatalog` returned `[]` for a provider whose listing threw; the
  reason reached only the log (`compatible: listing voices failed: fetch
  failed`).
- Fish Audio with no list fetched this session does not throw: it
  answers `[mul/default]` plus a `stale` notice whose detail already says
  `cannot reach api.fish.audio`, and only the settings voice browser read
  notices.
- The remote interface's `{ error: 'network' }` path, Zotero's own
  connection message, is reached only when the whole catalog rejects,
  which `Promise.allSettled` never does: an offline listing answered
  `{ voices: {} }`.

Since 1.16.10 a failed provider stays in the catalog as an entry with no
voices and a `failure` (`key` for `auth`/`no-key`, `unreachable`
otherwise, the 15 s bound included). Every consumer already handled an
entry with zero voices, which an empty Fish Speech references folder
gives, and `buildVoicesResponse` builds one config per voice, so neither
the voice browser nor the response sent to Zotero changed.
`unlistedProviders` (catalog.ts) adds Fish's `stale` entries as
unreachable; getVoices hands the list to `reconcile` as `unlisted`, and
memory-sync words the announcement by it at open and at the activation
guard. `diagnostics.readAloudMemory()`'s `substitution.cause` says which
path ran.

Two things seen on the way. The "said twice" of the issue is one toast
on screen: `showToast` reuses one element per document and restarts its
timer (`src/ui/speed-toast.ts:53-66`), so an open followed at once by
play shows one message; the guard's own announcement is kept so a later
press of play still says why nothing plays. And the resync that
activation runs plans against the manager's own list, so with that list
empty it clears the substitution record: read `substitution` after the
open, not after play, when nothing else is listed.

Not covered: a document voice that is one of Zotero's own voices, opened
while Zotero's servers cannot be reached, still gets the generic
message — `nativeVoices` returns null without saying why.
