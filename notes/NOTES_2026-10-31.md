## A language cue does not steady Fish Speech on a slash; a delivery cue does (2026-10-31 13:41, issue #99)

ADR 0002 held #99 for a language source, on the premise that naming the
language fixes a Fish Speech voice's short text, as it did for Fish Audio
(ADR 0001). That premise rested on one sample per arm (2026-09-13 12:58).
Measured again on the owner's H200 server, outside Zotero, with the
plugin's saved Fish Speech URL and Extra headers read from `prefs.js` and
the provider's request body (`format: mp3`, `normalize: true`,
`use_memory_cache: on`):

- **The loop.** `seed` in `/v1/tts` makes the reply byte-identical (two
  seeds re-requested); production sends none, so its audio varies. Whisper
  large-v3-turbo (MLX) transcribes each clip in forced English; a reading of
  ` 2/50 HP ` counts as right when it starts with two/2, an optional
  slash/of/over, fifty/50, then HP. On the ten clips the owner judged by ear
  on 2026-09-13 (cloud and Local), the rule agrees 10/10, and Whisper writes
  the owner's own descriptions: `cnxp`, `250th GP`. Local's wrong clip is
  `TWiF 50 HP`.
- **Rates.** Reference `bella`, ` 2/50 HP `, seeds 1–64 per arm: bare
  20/64 wrong (31%); `[Speak in American English]` 16/64 (25%);
  `[Speak naturally]` 3/64 (5%). Two-sided Fisher: bare vs English
  p = 0.56; bare vs natural p = 0.00013; English vs natural p = 0.0021.
  14 of the English cue's 16 failures fall on seeds where bare also fails:
  the language cue barely moves the sampling. Every failure is the same
  blur of "two" into the slash (`TWE`, `Tui`, `TWiF`), and Whisper's own
  language detection hears English in all of them, so the wrong audio is
  not another language. No transcript of some 200 prefixed clips contains
  the prefix.
- **Minimal repro.** `2/50` alone, seed 11: `TWE 50`; `2 50`, same seed:
  right (4/4 seeds). Spacing the slash (`2 / 50`) still fails. The spaces
  bracket removal leaves and `HP` are not needed for the failure.
- **Normalization is not involved.** `normalize: false` returns
  byte-identical audio to `true` for all 16 seeds tried: this server
  ignores the field.
- **`xiaobei`** (the server's other reference) reads ` 2/50 HP ` as
  Chinese-sounding audio, bare and with the English cue alike (0/8 by the
  English rule; Whisper detects `zh`). The English cue does not move it
  to English.
- **Limits.** One phrase, mainly one reference, one server whose model is
  not reported (`/` serves Kuí's OpenAPI page, no version). `100 exp` was
  right 8/8 bare. The fault is the model's, not the request's: the plugin
  sends the right characters. Whisper is a proxy for the ear, calibrated on
  ten clips; a human listen remains the final check.
- **What it changes.** Even a perfect language source would only feed a
  cue that does not help here, so the question ADR 0002 waits on does not
  decide this bug. Fish Audio's own cue was also settled on one sample per
  arm and was not re-measured (its account is the owner's).

Loop, jobs, audio and transcripts: `.tmp/fish-speech-pronunciation/`
in the issue99 worktree (git-ignored); its README gives the three
commands. No plugin code, preference or Zotero state was touched.

*Widened at 15:01 (below): the delivery cue helps this one stat and
worsens two others; it is not a fix.*

## Fish Audio reads both stats bare now, and its `language` field reads 2/50 as a fraction (2026-10-31 14:04, issue #99)

The same loop on Fish Audio, at the owner's request: Dax (the voice of
#98), `s2.1-pro-free`, the production route
`/v1/tts/stream/with-timestamp` and body (`mp3`, 64 kbps, `latency: normal`,
no `normalize`, so its default `true`). The cloud takes no `seed`; each
arm is independent draws, and the eight pilot clips hashed differently, so
nothing was served from a cache. Before the run the free model was still
listed at price `"0"` in the keyless `/compat/v1/models`, and API credit
was `0.000000` before, every 20 requests and after: a paid fallback
(issue #181) would have answered 402. All 200 requests answered 200.

| Text sent | ` 2/50 HP ` right | ` 100 exp` right |
| --- | --- | --- |
| bare | 32/32 | 16/16 |
| `[Speak in American English]` + text (ADR 0001, shipping) | 32/32 | 15/16 |
| `[Speak naturally]` + text | 31/32 | 16/16 |
| text, `language: "en"` | 11/32 | 16/16 |

- **#98's fault does not reproduce today.** On 2026-09-13 bare Dax read
  `cnxp` and `250th GP` (one clip each, plus the two cached originals);
  now 32/32 and 16/16 are right bare. The shipping cue neither helps nor
  harms measurably.
- **`openapi.json` now documents `language`** on the timestamp route
  (read 2026-10-31; #98 found no selector on 2026-09-13). Its own text: used
  only by text normalization and word-timestamp alignment, "not passed to
  the speech model"; omitted, `normalize` detects the language per chunk.
  With `language: "en"`, 21/32 read `2 50th HP` (p = 6.3e-9 against
  bare): English normalization spells the slash as a fraction, "two
  fiftieths". That is the shape of September's `250th GP`, which suggests
  —not proves— that September's fault was the normalizer's and that it
  has changed since.
- **Local and cloud differ in kind.** The Fish Speech server ignores
  `normalize` (byte-identical either way), and its failure is the model
  blurring "two" into the slash, which the language cue does not touch.

Evidence: `fa-out0/`, `fa-out1/` and `fa-synth.mjs` beside the Local
loop in `.tmp/fish-speech-pronunciation/`.

## No request-level lever steadies Fish Speech across stats; some stats loop for a minute (2026-10-31 15:01, issue #99)

The owner asked to widen the Local test before choosing a fix. Per-phrase
rules were fixed before the run (`verdict2.mjs`); they check how a
transcript starts, so clip length is checked separately for loops. `bella`,
seeds 1–32 per arm unless noted; right readings out of 32:

| Text | bare (server default) | `[Speak naturally]` | temperature 0.5 | temperature 0.3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ` 2/50 HP ` | 24 (44/64 over seeds 1–64) | 61/64 over seeds 1–64 | 21 | 18 |
| ` 120/120 HP ` | 25 | 14 | 22 | 28 |
| ` 1,250 gold ` | 17 | 7 | 20 | 19 |
| ` +15 STR ` | 24 | 24 | 23 | 30 |
| ` 13/40 MP ` | 32 | 32 | — | 16/16 |
| ` Status Window ` | 32 | 32 | — | 16/16 |

- **The delivery cue does not generalize.** It worsens ` 1,250 gold `
  (p = 0.019) and ` 120/120 HP ` (p = 0.0097) as much as it helps
  ` 2/50 HP `. The wrong readings: `1,150 gold`, `100-120 HP`, `PLOS 15
  STR` / `FLIST 15 STR` for "plus".
- **Temperature is no lever either.** The server's sampling defaults are
  temperature 0.8 and top_p 0.8 (sending either value returns
  byte-identical audio to sending none; 0.7 does not). Against the default
  at the same seeds, 0.5 and 0.3 move each phrase by noise and in both
  directions (two-sided Fisher p from 0.082 to 1.0); 0.3 is worse on
  ` 2/50 HP `, better on ` +15 STR `.
- **Runaway loops.** ` 120/120 HP ` produced about a minute of audio
  ("100 and 100 and 100 …") in 2 of 32 clips bare, 2 of 32 with the cue
  and 2 of 32 at temperature 0.5; none of 32 at 0.3. A two-word stat read
  for a minute is a worse failure than a misreading, and it is detectable
  by length.
- **`xiaobei`** (16 seeds per arm, all six texts) reads the English stats
  with Chinese numerals and mostly garbled (`一二百五是够的` for
  ` 1,250 gold `, `ESTR` for ` +15 STR `), bare and cued alike; only
  ` Status Window ` reads right, 16/16 both ways.

Measured so far, the English cue, a delivery cue, `normalize` and
temperature each fail to give a consistent improvement across stats on
this server. The misreadings belong to the model reading short numeric
text without context; nothing found in the request steadies it.

## Zotero spells a Segment with a newline per `<br>` and an image's text per `<img>`, which the page does not hold (2026-10-31 22:47, issue #209)

Read in the installed Zotero 10.0.6 `omni.ja`, matching what #209 read in
11.0-beta.1. The document worker's `walkInline`
(`resource/document-worker/worker.js:165304-165329`) pushes `{ text: '\n' }`
for a `<br>` and `getImageText(img)` for an inline `<img>`, neither with an
anchor. `getImageText` (`:165235-165254`) answers the `aria-label`, else the
`alt`, unless the alt has no white space and looks like a URL, a path or an
image file name. `addSegment` collapses white space (`normalizeText`,
`resource/reader/reader.js:71752`), so the `<br>` becomes a space and the
alt text stays. The mappers build a sentence's range from its anchored text
nodes only (`SnapshotPositionMapper.textNodeSpansToSourcePosition` skips the
"Synthetic node (<br> newline, image alt text)", `reader.js:63096`), so
`range.toString()` lacks both.

The highlight patch's text check (#3, #4, #74) therefore refused a correctly
placed sentence: the stand-in primary painted transparent, the sentence
pieces dropped. It now asks `showsText`: `toString()` first, then the same
range spelled as Zotero spells a Segment (`src/read-aloud/range-text.ts`,
`segmentTextOf`). The walk reads `commonAncestorContainer`,
`intersectsNode`, the boundary points, `nodeType`, `childNodes`,
`localName`, `data` and `getAttribute` from the sandbox through the
reader's Xrays; unit tests run it over a fake DOM, and 1.16.16-beta4 is the
first build to run it live. Fixture: `test/fixtures/br-alt-text/`.

The Positions File is not on this path: `readSharedCapture` takes the
quotation from `activeSegment.text` and the SDT block, never from the page.
The same alt text does reach the phone, though: OpenReader's page walk
gives an `<img>` no text, so a short sentence whose image text outweighs
its own words falls under the anchor matcher's 0.7 word threshold there
(OpenReader #337). Checking that showed the plugin's copy of the matcher
behind OpenReader's #282/#283 fixes (#234).

## The EPUB view relabels every image "Zoom In", so an image's Segment text cannot be read back from the page (2026-10-31 23:32, issue #209)

1.16.16-beta4 read an image's text from the page (`aria-label`, else prose
`alt`, as the worker does) and failed live on the EPUB only: the primary
`#00000000` and no sentence pieces for an image paragraph, the stanza with
unspaced `<br/>` fine in both views. `zotero-tester` read the range's
nodes: every `<img>` in the EPUB view carries `aria-label="Zoom In"` and
the class `clickable-image`. Zotero 10.0.6 `resource/reader/reader.js:55590-55601`
(`sanitizeAndRender`, case `'img'`) sets both on every image not inside
`a, [*|type="frontmatter"]`, overwriting a source `aria-label`; the
document worker read the source file, so the Segment holds the source
label or alt. The snapshot view does not relabel.

So the walk no longer spells an image: `rangeParts` gives the range's text
with `'\n'` per `<br>` and a gap per `<img>`, and `rangeSpells` accepts a
Segment that holds the text pieces in order, first at its start, last at
its end, anything in the gaps; a range with no text outside its gaps
matches nothing. The walk over the live reader DOM from the sandbox raised
nothing on beta4 (no Permission denied, dead object or TypeError).

Seen on the same run, not this issue's: a snapshot's last paragraph,
selector `body > p:last-child`, resolves to nothing once the reader has
appended two `<div>`s to the body, so nothing is drawn for it.
