## Stable estimates use accumulated listening across transitions (2026-09-27 13:49, issue #152)

The implementation separates RemainingTime's raw estimate from two
RemainingTimeDisplay envelopes. Readiness requires three representative
clips and eight seconds of measured audio; eligibility uses eight
word-equivalent units from the existing mixed word/CJK weights and rejects
numeric-only text. Fully measured ranges bypass extrapolation readiness.
Known durations retain their prefix sums after clip eviction. A 16-sample
robust weighted pace replaces the unbounded cumulative ratio.

The display budget is based on accumulated source-clock progress divided
by playback rate plus elapsed configured gaps. Source stops, natural ends,
and gap completion/cancellation settle that progress independently of
Player refreshes. In stop(), the old playback offset must be retained until
source progress is settled; writing the new offset first would discard all
listening since the previous source start. A regression pauses between
queries and verifies both the estimate's decrease and one second of
accumulated listening. Pauses, held network responses and frozen context
clocks contribute no budget. Dropping a pending gap on pause changes raw
time but does not force an immediate displayed decrease.

Changed speed, intentional skip, fresh bind, handoff and replay reset the
envelopes. Same-speed writes, ordinary resume and controller carry-on do
not. Section endpoints key a separate envelope, capped by document output.
Diagnostics expose remainingCalibration and listeningTime alongside the
same remainingTime snapshot used by the Player. This permits live checks
of the actual correction allowance and calibration readiness.

The initial regression run failed on no-audio numbers, upward correction,
and paused recalibration. Focused tests then passed, including a minute of
varying decoded clips across natural sentence and gap boundaries. Existing
tests that expected a text-only number now expect Estimating until audio
arrives. Unit tests cover CJK, silence, short exact ranges, outliers, speed,
position, completion and clock stalls. Beta12 is the verification build;
its installed identity and live evidence belong on issue #152.
