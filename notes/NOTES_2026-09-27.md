## Consecutive outline headings replace outermost sections (2026-09-27 10:45, issue #150)

The owner replaced the outermost section of #148 with text ending at the
next outline heading at any depth. A parent's introduction ends at its
first child. `readingSections` walks `children` depth first by index, so
reader-realm arrays do not need sandbox callbacks. Parent/descendant
entries at the same mapped start collapse to the deepest title; duplicate
siblings, reversed refs, unresolved refs and segment-crossing boundaries
still suppress section time. Section lookup retains the identity cache
and binary search.

The display is one localized line with generic Doc/Section labels.
`floor(seconds / 60) + 1`, clamped to one, gives the strict minute bound:
zero before completion stays `<1 min`, and exactly 60 seconds is `<2 min`.
Completion still comes from the Engine's independent finished state.
Floating puts its 16 px line above controls and adds 20 px including the
gap (128/222 px collapsed/expanded). Both the host height and CSS changed;
bars keep 34 px and place time after volume in DOM order.

Local tests cover three outline depths, introductions, shared starts,
invalid descendants, and the real manager's nested section snapshots.
Live verification is pending; issue #150 will hold its observed values.
