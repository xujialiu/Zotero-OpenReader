## The reading line splits the free space, and a blank message is a missing one (2026-09-29 04:58, issue #155)

- **Geometry.** `followTarget` places a box with its top at
  `above + (VH - h) * line / 100`: the uncovered viewport's free space is
  split above and below it by the line. At 50 this is exactly the old
  `centerOn`, `(top + bottom) / 2 - VH / 2 - above`, so no earlier
  expectation moved. Two readings of "the sentence at 10%" were turned
  down: its middle at the line cuts a tall sentence's first line off near
  0%, and its top at the line makes 50% something other than centering.
  The split never cuts off a sentence that fits, at any value.
- **What it places.** The whole fitting sentence on entry, clipping and
  explicit return, and the real word of an oversized sentence. The
  oversized sentence's head still goes to the top edge plus
  `followMargin`. Positions the shadow cannot measure (a rotated page, a
  position without rects) still fall back to Zotero's own
  `navigateToPosition` with `block: 'center'`: that path has no line.
- **A change of the line re-places the current sentence** as a change of
  style does: the PDF shadow keys its per-view entry on `style@line`, the
  EPUB record keeps the line beside the style. Paginated EPUB flow leaves
  the line out of that comparison, since a line change must not turn a
  page. Both `inspect`s report `line`.
- **A Fluent message may not be empty in this pane.** English puts
  "% from the top" after the field and Chinese "距顶部" before it, so the
  row carries a message for each side. An English `{ "" }` for the side
  before failed `test/l10n.test.ts` — `paneElementBlank`, the same check
  `diagnostics.l10n()` runs on the live pane, reads an element Fluent left
  empty as untranslated. English's side before the field is "at" instead.
