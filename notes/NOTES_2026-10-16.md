## Fish Audio's models, and what its replies cannot say (2026-10-16 18:55, issues #181, #182)

Read from docs.fish.audio, its blog and changelog, keyless API calls, and
one free request with the owner's key (API credit 0).

**Five text-to-speech models** in the `model` header's enum of
`openapi.json`: `s1`, `s2-pro`, `s2.1-pro`, `s2.1-pro-free`, `drama-3`.
Prices: $15 per million UTF-8 bytes on `s1`, `s2-pro` and `s2.1-pro`;
`s2.1-pro-free` $0; `drama-3` $50 per million **characters**, $10 from its
launch on 2026-10-13 until 2026-11-12, and only in its launch post
(fish.audio/blog/fish-audio-drama-3/), not on the price page. Per
character against per byte: for Chinese, three bytes a character, drama-3
at its regular price costs about what `s2.1-pro` does; for English it
costs over three times as much. `s1` is deprecated (changelog, 2026-10-08)
and retires on 2026-12-31, after which it is served and billed as
`s2.1-pro`. drama-3: pronunciation right 93.2% against S2.1 Pro's 88.1%,
proper nouns and terms 95.9% against 87.3%; free-form `[bracket]`
directions and paired tags `<whisper>`, `<emphasis>`, `<soft>`, `<fast>`,
`<slow>`, `<stress>`, `<forceful>` — both stripped before a sentence is
sent while bracket removal is on (`core/speech-text.ts`, the default
pairs `<> []`).

**The free model's end date has moved four times**: July 24, the end of
July, August 31, and now "Free access is available through November 30,
2026 — we'll communicate any changes with advance notice"
(fish.audio/blog/s2-1-pro-free-api/, the changelog too). What the route
does after it is documented nowhere.

**Fish's own model list is the compat catalog**, keyless
`GET https://api.fish.audio/compat/v1/models` (also under
`/compat/openai/v1/` and `/compat/api/v1/`): five entries,
`fish-audio/s2.1-pro`, `…/s2.1-pro-free` (`pricing.prompt: "0"`,
"Free variant of S2.1 Pro for testing and prototyping; no production
latency guarantees."), `…/s2-pro`, `…/s1`, and `…/transcribe-1`, told
apart by `architecture.modality` (`text->speech` against `audio->text`).
**It lacks drama-3** three days after launch. `pricing.prompt` is a
number per unit without the unit: `0.000015` matches $15 per million
bytes, and nothing says what a per-character model would carry. The
native API has no list of models (`/v1/models` 404; `GET /model` lists
voices).

**An unknown model is a paid one.** `openapi.json`, of the `model`
header: "If omitted or set to an unrecognized value, the request falls
back to `s2.1-pro`." No request option makes it an error.

**A reply does not say which model answered.** `POST
/v1/tts/stream/with-timestamp`, `model: s2.1-pro-free`, text `Hello.`:
HTTP/2 200, headers `content-type: text/event-stream`,
`ratelimit-current-concurrency: 0`, `ratelimit-limit-concurrency: 5`,
`x-fish-trace-id`, `x-fishaudio-datacenter: hongkong`, and Cloudflare's
own; two events with only `audio_base64`, `content`, `alignment`,
`chunk_seq`, `chunk_audio_offset_sec` (the first `alignment` null, the
second `{ segments: [{ text: "Hello", start: 0, end: 0.56 }],
audio_duration: 0.743 }`). `openapi.json` documents no other header on a
200. `GET /wallet/self/api-credit` (Bearer key) answers `credit`,
`cumulative_top_up`, `has_free_credit` — a balance, no per-request usage;
the owner's read `credit: "0.0"`, and its `updated_at` moved with the free
request while the credit did not.

**What was built.** The Model dropdown (#182) and a check before every
request (#181): the plugin knows `s2.1-pro-free` (free through
2026-11-30), `s2.1-pro`, `drama-3` and `s2-pro`, and adds what the compat
list carries beyond them. A known paid model is read on that knowledge; the
free model while the list shows it at 0, or, when the list cannot be
read, through its announced date; a model only the list knew while the
list carries it; anything else is refused with `model-not-free` or
`model-gone`, which the Player words. The list is read with a 5-second
bound and kept 30 minutes (a failed read 5 minutes) in the provider's
session cache, which the pane shares. `fish.freeOnly` became `fish.model`:
an Off becomes `s2.1-pro` at the upgrade, in a backup and in the sync.
`cacheVersion()` gained `fish.model`; before, the free switch was not in
it, which did no harm while both of its models were the same S2.1 Pro.

**What the check cannot see**: a model the plugin knows that Fish retires
without notice and never put on the compat list (drama-3 today) is
answered and billed as `s2.1-pro`, unnoticed; its regular price is not
lower. The free model's check is only as current as the compat list.
