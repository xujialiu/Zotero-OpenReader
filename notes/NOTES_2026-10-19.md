# Zotero-OpenReader — engineering log, 2026-10-19

## Zotero resolves every Chinese region as one, so a language pick needs the plugin's voice (2026-10-19 01:40, issue #223)

The Player and the voice browser filed a voice under Zotero's normalized
language. In the installed reader bundle (Zotero 11.0,
`resource/reader/reader.js`), `normalizeLanguage` (38103) maps the
regions `CN`, `HK` and `TW` to none and `XA` and `SA` to `001`. Every
place the manager groups or resolves voices goes through it:
`getSupportedLanguages` (39370), `getVoicesForLanguage` and
`getVoiceRegion` (39383-39390), the manager's `languages`,
`currentVoiceRegion` and `voicesForLanguage` (84726-84737),
`_findFallbackVoice` (84905), and the region `selectTier` carries over
(84782). Splitting the Player's list was therefore only half of it.
Asked for Chinese (Hong Kong), the manager resolves among all of the
entry's Chinese voices. Its first step takes the remembered tier voice,
and its region step compares a requested `HK` with a voice region that is
always none. So it lands on the remembered Mandarin voice, or on the
first Chinese voice it lists. A provider switch carries no region for a
Chinese voice, so Hong Kong is lost there too.

What the plugin's own lists had been showing, measured the same day:

- **Dialects were already separate.** `zh-CN-liaoning` normalizes to
  region `CN-liaoning`, which no table drops, so Azure's six dialects were
  their own languages before this change. ICU named them
  `Chinese (China, LIAONING)`.
- **Zotero's own Arabic.** `api.zotero.org/tts/voices?format=2`
  (2026-10-18) has Standard in 9 locales and Premium in 53. Chinese is only
  `zh-CN` (4 Standard voices, 32 Premium). Premium's Arabic is `ar-XA`
  (25 voices) and `ar-SA` (2). Azure's Saudi voices (`ar-SA`) were named
  `Arabic (world)`, because `SA` became `001` beside Azure's other Arabic
  regions.
- **macOS 27's `say -v '?'`** lists Eddy, Flo, Grandma and Grandpa under
  both `zh_CN` and `zh_TW`. Merged, each was two identical names under one
  Chinese.

The fix files each voice under its locale as sent (`language-dropdown.ts`),
and the Player takes its languages from `manager.voices` rather than
`manager.languages`. The language pick
(`memory-sync.ts stageLanguagePick`) now models Zotero's pool with a port
of `isLanguageSupported`. Where `normalizeLanguage` loses the picked
locale's region (`zoteroKeepsRegion`), it stages a voice of that locale
unless the remembered voice is already one. `stageTierPick` does the same
for a provider switch. The old fake manager in `memory-sync.test.ts`
compared raw regions, so it could not show the Mandarin landing. It now
resolves through the normalization. The new cases fail without the
staging, and two of them only once the list order makes Zotero's
first-of-the-pool fallback wrong.

ICU facts the names rely on (Node 22, ICU 78.2):

- `Intl.DisplayNames` with `style: 'short'` names `HK` and `MO` as
  `Hong Kong` / `Macao`, `香港` / `澳门` in zh-CN. It also shortens `US` to
  `US` and `GB` to `UK`, so only those two regions take the short form.
- English's short name for the script `Cans` is `UCAS`, and its long one
  `Unified Canadian Aboriginal Syllabics`. OpenReader writes `Syllabics`.
- ICU names the region `XA` `Pseudo-Accents` (`伪地区`): it is Google's
  world Arabic, so it is named as `001`.
- `Intl.getCanonicalLocales` turns `zh-344` into `zh-HK` and `sh-RS` into
  `sr-Latn-RS`, and throws on `en_US`. `Intl.Locale#maximize` gives
  `zh-HK` Hant, `sr-ME` Latn and `iu-CA` Cans.
- ICU has no names for subdivisions, so a dialect's place cannot come from
  it in Chinese. Azure's six are Fluent messages (`ztts-place-*`).

`ui/player.ts` publishes a snapshot every 250 ms for every attached tab,
and naming Azure's 155 locales takes about 9 ms on the first call in Node.
`languageNames` keeps its last 32 answers; a repeated call takes 0.015 ms.
