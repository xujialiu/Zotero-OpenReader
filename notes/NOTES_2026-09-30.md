  448) or a new session clears the set too. Within one 250 ms snapshot the

  alerts, "Play resumes once time is bought"), and led to the tier being
  switched off instead.

  260 to 259 credits during the run with no Zotero audio played, so a
  figure read in one script is not the next one's.
- **A first Play in a session started minimized lags**: 4.6 s once, with
  `the new voice audio output is blocked`; on a settled session every
  alert opened within 653 ms.

## A Zotero tier that runs out is switched off after its players close, and the Engine reports only a refusal that fails playback (2026-09-30 01:40, issue #140)

- **Where a refusal is seen.** Zotero's two account words,
  `quota-exceeded` and `daily-limit-exceeded`, arrive in the Engine's
  `fetchFor` (`read-aloud/engine/index.ts`). A read-ahead's refusal is not
  final (the session asks once more when playback reaches it), and acting
  on it would close the player while the sentence already paid for is
  still playing. So the Engine reports a refusal of the reading's own voice
  only through the session's `failed` notice, and a refusal of any other
  voice at once: that is a voice being switched to, whose handoff then
  fails while the old voice reads on. In the unit harness the switch's
  fetch comes after the pick's own short wait: 0 ms of virtual time
  was not enough, 200 ms was.
- **The plugin's own figure stops the request.** A Zotero voice whose
  `minutesRemaining` (the tier's credits over its price, reader.js
  39256-39263) is 0 or less is answered `quota-exceeded` without asking
  Zotero. The figure comes from the voice list's headers and the manager's
  60 s `tts/credits` poll, so after the credits reach 0 at most one more
  request can go out before the figure says so.
- **The decision reads the credits again** (`tts/credits`, free), falling
  back on the Engine's figure: 0 switches the tier off; above 0 (Zotero
  refused a dearer voice than what is left) only reminds. The daily limit
  switches off whatever is left. `diagnostics.zoteroRefusals()` reports the
  last one.
- **Close first, then switch.** The reading guard (issue #121) refuses to
  switch a tier off while any open player, paused ones included, holds one
  of its voices. The switch-off therefore closes those players first, the
  stop key's way (`toggleReadAloudPopup(false)`, positions kept), then
  writes `zotero-<tier>.enabled` false, which the voice lists and the
  pane's rows already follow. A reader counts when its manager's `_voice`
  or `selectedTier` is the tier; a voice being switched to does not, since
  the pick leaves `selectedVoiceID` alone until it commits
  (`voice-pick.ts` `valid`).
- **The reminder cannot live in the Player**, whose player is closed by
  then: it is a box of its own in `toastDoc(reader)`, the reader's
  document or the main window when the tab is behind another, and it goes
  at shutdown with the speed toast.
- **Enable reads the credits too**: a tier at 0 stays off with "No
  remaining time on Premium. Add more time first." Credits that cannot be
  read, or that Zotero gives as null, do not block it.

## The guard's Close and continue closes by handle, and Enable never counted for much (2026-09-30 10:08, issue #160)

- **The #71 stop-every-player question had been unreachable since #121.**
  Every caller of `refuseWhileReading` passes a proposal and the pane always
  wires `affectedTabs`, so the branch holding `askToStop` / `stopReading`
  ran only in the callers' unit tests, which built rows without the impact
  check. It is gone; a row without the check now falls back to every open
  player, refusal only.
- **The close works on the reader handles the question listed, not on a
  recomputed list.** The pane's `html:dialog` is modal over the preferences
  window only: reader windows keep playing, so a tab can start reading with
  the same provider while the question is up. Recomputing at the press would
  close it unlisted; the re-check after the close names it instead.
  `settings-impact` hands the sessions out with their reader
  (`affectedSessions`), `src/index.ts` pairs each title with
  `playerStop.close(reader)`, the headphone button's `toggleReadAloudPopup(false)`
  on that one reader.
- **Enable was refused in one case only**: an open player whose voice
  `settings-impact` cannot place (`uncertain`), through `editsPlayerList`.
  A `<switch>.enabled` key whose new value is true is now dropped before any
  session is weighed, so the Enable button, a restore and the background sync
  all let it through; the lists refresh through #121's staged refresh.
- **`diagnostics.readingImpact()` would have thrown** once sessions carried
  their reader: `JSON.stringify` of a Zotero reader is circular. The
  diagnostic strips `reader` before it serializes.

## A pause sends both positions files up at once: the ten quiet seconds handed the phone its own old place (2026-09-30 18:53, issue #161)

- **The gap was the plugin's own timer, not the network.** Since 1.13.2
  (#126) a pause or a player close armed `PAUSE_SYNC_QUIET_MS = 10_000`
  in `src/index.ts` before poking either transport, while OpenReader syncs
  at once on its own pause and pulls exactly once before Play (bounded at
  two seconds, no polling). A phone picked up inside those ten seconds read
  the file before the desktop's sentence was in it and played from its own
  older place. Reading on there stamped that place newer than the desktop's
  pre-pause sentence, and the newest-stamp merge (spec 6.7) then carried the
  stale continuation to the desktop too.
- **The quiet period bought nothing the transports did not already do.**
  `core/single-flight.ts` folds any burst of pokes into the run in flight
  plus one trailing run, and an unchanged file costs a GET and no PUT, so a
  burst of pauses is at most two round trips per file. `syncAfterPause`
  now pokes both transports from the sampler's pause callback directly; the
  timer, its handle and its cancel at shutdown are gone.
- **The cross-product loop that proved it.** The harness in
  `.tmp/issue-161/handoff.test.ts` (a copy of the diagnosis's
  `/tmp/openreader-sync-104/`; gitignored, never committed) runs the real
  sampler, the pause callback lifted out of `src/index.ts` with the
  TypeScript AST, the real shared transport and OpenReader's real
  transport, adoption planner and stamp against an in-memory file, run from
  the OpenReader checkout (`npx vitest run --config …/vitest.config.mts`,
  about 1.3 s). On the old code the Play pull at pause +1 s adopted nothing
  and the phone's continuation reached the desktop at +10 s: 3 of 4 red.
  On the fix all 4 are green, including a foreground pull at +1 s that
  already finds the desktop's sentence.
- **The regression test sits at the whole plugin**, `test/index.test.ts`:
  the real `startup()` against the stand-in Zotero, the sync switch on, an
  in-memory WebDAV folder behind the global `fetch`, a PDF reader whose
  manager goes from speaking to paused. Both files must be requested within
  one second of the pause; the old code requested neither for ten.
- **What it costs.** A resume within about a second of a pause may wait for
  that pause's run to end before its own pull starts (single flight); the
  wait stays under `RESUME_PULL_MS`, two seconds, as before.
- **What it does not close.** A phone picked up faster than the sampler's
  tick (at most 500 ms) plus one GET and one PUT still reads the old file.
  Closing that would take behavior on the phone's side.
- Not verified live: the owner waived the zotero-tester run for this
  change. Item 7 of `test/zotero-dev/cases/document-positions.md` holds the
  new expected output for the next run.
