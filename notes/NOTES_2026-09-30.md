## The guard's Close and continue closes by handle, and Enable never counted for much (2026-09-30 10:08, issue #160)

- **The #71 stop-every-player question had been unreachable since #121.**
  Every caller of `refuseWhileReading` passes a proposal and the pane always
  wires `affectedTabs`, so the branch holding `askToStop` / `stopReading`
  ran only in the callers' unit tests, which built rows without the impact
  check. It is gone; a row without the check now falls back to every open
  player, refusal only.
- **The close works on the reader handles the question listed, not on a
  recomputed list.** The pane's `html:dialog` is modal over the preferences
  window only: reader windows keep playing, so a tab can start reading with
  the same provider while the question is up. Recomputing at the press would
  close it unlisted; the re-check after the close names it instead.
  `settings-impact` hands the sessions out with their reader
  (`affectedSessions`), `src/index.ts` pairs each title with
  `playerStop.close(reader)`, the headphone button's `toggleReadAloudPopup(false)`
  on that one reader.
- **Enable was refused in one case only**: an open player whose voice
  `settings-impact` cannot place (`uncertain`), through `editsPlayerList`.
  A `<switch>.enabled` key whose new value is true is now dropped before any
  session is weighed, so the Enable button, a restore and the background sync
  all let it through; the lists refresh through #121's staged refresh.
- **`diagnostics.readingImpact()` would have thrown** once sessions carried
  their reader: `JSON.stringify` of a Zotero reader is circular. The
  diagnostic strips `reader` before it serializes.
