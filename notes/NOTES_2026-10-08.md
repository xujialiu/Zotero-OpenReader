## README screenshots out of the running Zotero on macOS (2026-10-08 23:04, issues #78, #141)

The four README screenshots were re-shot through the bridge on macOS
(Zotero 10.0.6-beta.4, OpenReader 1.16.8), the way
[2026-08-28](NOTES_2026-08-28.md) did on Windows. What differs on a Mac:

- **Scale 2 is real resolution here.** The display's device pixel ratio
  is 2, so the PDF page's canvas is already rendered at twice its CSS
  size, and
  `win.browsingContext.currentWindowGlobal.drawSnapshot(new win.DOMRect(x, y, w, h), 2, bg)`
  returns sharp text for the page and the chrome alike — none of the
  2026-08-28 zoom-and-widen tricks were needed.
- **The settings window is reachable this way.** `zotero_screenshot`
  cannot target it ([2026-09-20](NOTES_2026-09-20.md)), but `drawSnapshot`
  on `Services.wm.getMostRecentWindow('zotero:pref')`'s own browsing
  context paints it. The group to crop is the heading's
  `closest('groupbox')`; a voice browser list's overflow makes a
  descendant scan for the bottom edge useless, so crop afterwards in
  ffmpeg.
- **An inactive window paints inactive chrome.** With the terminal in
  front, the reader toolbar came out mid-gray. `mw.focus()` from the bridge
  does not activate the app (`Services.focus.activeWindow` stayed another
  window); `osascript -e 'tell application "Zotero" to activate'` does,
  and the previous front app (System Events' frontmost process) is
  activated again right after.
- **A resize while paused drops the highlight.** Narrowing the window
  (1120×820, the sidebar closed, so the Top bar's labels stay legible at
  720 px in the README) left the paused sentence unhighlighted until a
  brief resume and pause redrew it.
- **Opening the player moves a document's stored position.**
  `toggleReadAloudPopup(true)` on the NeuralSurv paper began at the first
  sentence of the stored sentence's paragraph, and the stored position
  followed it. It was put back by `manager.jumpTo()` with the stored
  sentence's own `sourcePosition` (found in `manager._segments` by its
  text), a pause, and closing the player: the stored point read
  `[433.2, 158.7]` on page 1 again, exactly as before.
- **Playing a capture costs sound.** A playing Player has to show a
  nonzero volume, so the plugin's volume stayed at 100 for the one
  capture taken while playing (under a second); every other step ran at
  volume 0.
- **The PNGs shrink by half with a palette**
  (`palettegen=max_colors=200:stats_mode=full`, `paletteuse=dither=none`)
  at 61–68 dB PSNR against the full-color crop, which no eye tells apart.
