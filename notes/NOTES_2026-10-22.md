## An entry or language pick is a draft beside the manager, and a pending switch is the Player's voice (2026-10-22 00:46, issues #224, #225)

Until #224 the Player's entry and language picks called the manager's
`selectTier` and `setLanguage`, and both resolve a voice at once. In the
10.0.3 manager (`test/fixtures/engine/read-aloud-manager-10.0.3.js`)
`setLanguage` clears `_voiceID` and runs `_resolveVoice`, and `selectTier`
takes `_persistedVoices.tierVoices[tier]` and then `_resolveVoice`, whose
fallback is the tier's or language's remembered voice, else the first of
the region, else the first of the language. While playing, voice-pick.ts
ran that resolution as a dry run and sent the voice it landed on through
the Handoff. The fields were put back, so the Player's three fields kept
the old choice until the takeover. That is what the owner saw as "the
first voice, after a while".

What the build found:

- **There is no way to keep an empty voice inside the manager.** Every
  voice-list landing (`loadVoices`), every `applyPersistedVoices`, and the
  memory sync's restores run `_resolveVoice` again. So the pick is a draft
  kept beside the manager (voice-pick.ts `choose`), which keeps its voice,
  paused, until a voice of the draft is picked.
- **A fresh selection needs the fields set first.** `_applyVoice` drops a
  voice that is not in `voicesForLanguage`. That getter filters by
  `_selectedTier` and by `_lang` plus `currentVoiceRegion ?? _region`.
  For `zh-HK`, `currentVoiceRegion` is none (`normalizeLanguage` drops
  `HK`), so `_region` must say `HK`. The commit writes `_selectedTier`,
  `_lang` and `_region` from the voice and then calls the manager's own
  `selectVoice`. While paused, the rebuild binds the Engine `started`,
  and the Engine asks nothing before Play.
- **`deactivate` already carries two own-property hooks**: the memory
  sync's lifecycle hook and the live voice list's. A third shadow, undone
  out of order, could put back a hook that had since been replaced. So the
  draft ends when it is next read (`draftOf`): a manager that was active
  and is no longer has stopped. Each of its four refusing shadows (`play`,
  `retry`, `jumpTo`, `repositionTo`) asks `draftOf` first, so an ended
  draft never refuses a later Play.
- **One engine test used `selectTier` to start a switch** (#140: a refused
  Zotero voice switched to while reading). It now picks the Premium voice
  itself.
- **The Player page's test DOM gave the pickers no `dataset.pick`.**
  `openPicker(button, undefined)` still listed the voices, because
  `choices()` falls through to `voices` for an unknown key. So every voice
  list test passed by accident, and the first provider-list test failed.
- **A cancel the user asked for and one they did not look the same to
  the Handoff.** Both reach voice-pick.ts as `cancelled`. A `WeakSet` of
  handoffs called off on purpose tells them apart: a newer pick, a re-pick
  of the voice reading, an entry or language pick, a detach. The others
  become `called-off` for the Player's notice. So does a switch dropped
  when `bind` sees other sentences: the parked switch's cancel in
  `keptSwitch`. A cancel after the commit, from `watchStart`, stays quiet.

## Verified live on 1.16.13-beta4, and a fourth argument the wiring dropped (2026-10-22 02:32, issues #224, #225)

Zotero 11 on macOS, locale en-US, System voices and Fish Audio's entry,
the fixture muted. Two runs: beta3 (c204bd4) and beta4 (70425b3).

- **The two-line notice was one line on beta3.** voice-pick.ts passed the
  voice reading as `notice`'s fourth argument. `startVoicePick` in
  `index.ts` wired it as `(reader, kind, voice) => voiceNotices?.notice(reader, kind, voice)`,
  so the toast read only "Preparing voice: B". TypeScript does not object
  when a callback with fewer parameters forwards fewer arguments. The unit
  tests called voice-pick and the notices separately, never through
  `index.ts`. The wiring now forwards `...args`. On beta4 the notice reads
  `Reading voice: A` and `Preparing voice: B`, a line each, from the first
  pending tick. It stayed up for as long as the switch was pending: a probe
  at 0.5× held one pending 6.75 s, and at t+5 s the notice still showed
  at opacity 1.
- **Every other item passed on both builds.** On a language pick while
  reading, the reading paused within the same tick. The voice menu opened
  by itself with only the picked locale's rows (System en-GB 9, Fish en-GB
  14), and the language menu opened when the new entry lacked the locale
  (Fish en-NZ, then System). The old voice asked for nothing while the
  draft stood (`requests` flat). Shift+Space and the Player's Play were
  refused, and ArrowRight moved the position one sentence, still paused. A
  voice picked from the draft wrote a `manual` document record and asked
  for nothing until Play. Play then read the paused sentence from offset 0
  in that voice. The voice keys took the first and last voice of the
  draft's list. Closing the Player under a draft restored the old entry,
  language and voice, and left the record byte-identical. With #225 the
  Player's voice field moved to the picked voice at 38 ms. A newer pick
  replaced the pending one with no called-off notice. A speed change
  showed "The switch to … was called off." for 5 s, and the Player went
  back to the old voice.
- **Zotero 11 gates a script-started AudioContext.** Without
  `notifyUserGestureActivation()` on the reader iframe's document, the
  context stays `suspended` at 0, with "An AudioContext was prevented from
  starting automatically" in the console. The first beta3 tester took this
  for a frozen machine. The kits now activate before every scripted Play.
- **The machine's audio output for new sessions died at about 18:22 UTC**
  in the beta4 run. Fresh sessions came up `suspended` at 0, with no
  media-sink error. Every audio-driven check had already passed with
  advancing clocks, so only the setup script's probe re-runs failed.
- **The first beta3 tester hit pi-subagents' 30-minute default.** A fresh
  one with a 90-minute bound finished from the snapshot in
  `Zotero.ZoteroTTSRun.state.ep224`, as on #223.
- **What only the owner can judge**: whether the menu that opens after
  the pick feels right in the Bottom bar and the Floating panel. Only the
  Top bar was driven.

## The Player's dropdowns kept their first version's fixed widths (2026-10-22 11:57, issue #227)

Since the Player's first version (8b49e0b, 2026-09-16), the Top and Bottom
bar sized the provider and language dropdowns at a flat 105 px
(`flex: 0 0 auto`) and the voice dropdown at `flex: 0 1 180px` with a
95 px floor. So only the voice dropdown moved with the window, and only
between 95 and 180 px.

- **A dropdown's own parts take 28 px**: padding 6 + 6, border 1 + 1, the
  gap 6 and the chevron 8. A 105 px dropdown leaves 77 px for the name.
- **Names measured at 13 px in the macOS system font** (a canvas in
  Chrome; Gecko may differ by a pixel or two): English (United States)
  139, OpenAI Compatible 118, Zotero Premium 97, Xiaomi MiMo 78, Cloudflare
  62. The longest language names `languageNames` composes from Azure's
  locales in English are Arabic (United Arab Emirates) and Spanish
  (Dominican Republic), 29 and 28 characters, 181 px each.
- **The fix is one rule**: `.field { flex: 1 1 0; min-width: 7.3em;
  max-width: 16.5em; }`. Flexible lengths are resolved before auto
  margins, so the layout button's `margin-left: auto` gets room only once
  all three are at the cap. The em sizes follow `--preview-font-size`,
  which `ui/player.ts` copies from Zotero's `.read-aloud-popup`. The
  Floating panel's `.layout-B .field` resets the floor and the cap.
- **The hover text was already there**: `player-controls.js` titles each
  dropdown `<Field>: <whole name>`.
- **Vitest has no layout engine**, so `test/ui/player-css.test.ts` pins
  the rules, and the widths are checked live
  (`test/zotero-dev/cases/player-dropdown-widths.md`).
